# Domain and release readiness

Do not purchase or hard-code a domain during the current verification phase. Choose one after BM's name, logo, business contact information and client review are approved, and before final production/store preparation.

A domain is not required to compile or upload an app. It is strongly recommended for a privacy policy, terms, support, account-deletion page, professional support email and brand credibility. Planned public paths can include `/`, `/privacy`, `/terms`, `/support` and `/delete-account`; email addresses can use the approved domain, such as `support@…` and `orders@…`.

Release blockers before Play Store/App Store work are: development Firebase live verification, production Firebase separation, approved identifiers, real OTP/order/admin/FCM/Excel tests, approved logo/icon/contact information, privacy and account-deletion content, and signing/store-account decisions. No store submission is part of Phase 5.5.
